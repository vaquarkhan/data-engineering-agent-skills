# Plan: ESG Regulatory Reporting Foundation

## Architecture

- define reporting framework, scope, and materiality logic
- map KPIs to sources, owners, and methodology records
- add regional compliance overlays for storage, transfer, access, and evidence

## Risks

- frameworks and regional controls conflict without a clear owner
- source evidence is too weak for assurance
- value-chain inputs are used with no provenance or confidence rating

## Verification

- methodology and lineage review
- regional control review
- assurance-readiness review
