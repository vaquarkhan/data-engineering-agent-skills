# Spec: ESG Regulatory Reporting Foundation

## Objective

Create a governed reporting foundation for sustainability and regulatory disclosures with traceable metrics, regional control overlays, and assurance-ready evidence.

## Reporting Surface

- ESG or sustainability KPIs
- regional privacy and sovereignty constraints
- source documents, factor libraries, and methodology records

## Quality Rules

- each KPI must map to a named owner and source
- methodology changes must be versioned
- published outputs must be reproducible from retained evidence

## Success Criteria

- reporting scope and framework are explicit
- source-to-report lineage is clear
- controls satisfy both reporting and regional compliance expectations
