# Privacy Retention Deletion Checklist

Use this checklist when handling sensitive data, retention, and deletion workflows.

## Classification

- [ ] Sensitive fields are classified
- [ ] Retention obligations are defined
- [ ] Legal hold or exception rules are documented

## Coverage

- [ ] All material copies and downstream stores are mapped
- [ ] Retention enforcement covers raw, transformed, and published layers
- [ ] Deletion propagation is explicit

## Evidence

- [ ] Compliance actions can be audited
- [ ] Manual steps are minimized or documented clearly
