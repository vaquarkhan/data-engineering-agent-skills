# ESG And Sustainability Reporting Checklist

Use this checklist when sustainability or ESG reporting must be built as an audit-ready data product.

## Scope And Framework

- [ ] Reporting framework is named explicitly
- [ ] Legal-entity and consolidation scope are defined
- [ ] Materiality, relevance, or disclosure thresholds are documented

## Data Foundations

- [ ] Each KPI is mapped to source systems and owners
- [ ] Value-chain or supplier data is tagged with provenance and confidence level
- [ ] Estimation, proxy, and fallback rules are documented

## Controls And Evidence

- [ ] Methodology, factors, and calculation rules are versioned
- [ ] Every reported datapoint has traceable source evidence
- [ ] Review, approval, and change-log controls are in place
- [ ] Reporting snapshots can be reproduced after publication

## Assurance Readiness

- [ ] Reconciliation or reasonableness checks exist
- [ ] Narratives and metrics are aligned to the same versioned reporting set
- [ ] Audit or assurance owners know where evidence lives

## Common Regional Signals

| Region | Typical framework signals | Engineering focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Europe` | `CSRD`, `ESRS`, double materiality, assurance, structured electronic reporting | materiality register, source traceability, versioned methodologies, audit-grade controls |
| `USA` | issuer, sector, lender, or investor sustainability disclosures | evidence quality, finance alignment, control maturity, defensible calculations |
| `India` | `BRSR` and `BRSR Core` for relevant listed-company reporting | KPI ownership, assurance-ready metrics, value-chain data quality, annual report alignment |
| `Saudi Arabia` | market, sector, lender, or supervisory sustainability and governance expectations | localized evidence, governance ownership, regulated-sector control overlays where applicable |
